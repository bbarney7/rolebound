## Design Context

The owner explicitly requests a complete unconstrained redesign and new name/icon. Audience and use cases are established in the conversation: local job seekers using a supervised pi agent with their own provider/CAPTCHA keys, profile/resume, discovery and tracking. The owner grants creative leadership; the selected brand voice is decisive, candid, optimistic.

Rolebound is the working name. Use a new horizontal workspace architecture, graphite/citron identity, cool pale surfaces, characterful sans typography and original folded-path R mark. Avoid the previous sidebar dashboard, forest green, Georgia headings and nested cards. Prioritize the next meaningful action, visible agent control, intentional readable forms and accessible mobile use. Both light and dark themes and reduced motion are required.
